# ETIS Roadmap

**Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems (ETIS)**  
**Software Engineering, Governance, and Operational Trust in the AI Era**

---

## Purpose of This Roadmap

This roadmap records the evolution of ETIS from a book-centered publication into a connected professional engineering ecosystem.

It identifies:

- what has been completed;
- what is currently active;
- what remains planned;
- how future additions should relate to the established ETIS architecture;
- which boundaries must remain stable as the ecosystem grows.

The roadmap is directional rather than speculative. New work should strengthen ETIS without duplicating completed capabilities, fragmenting the framework, or weakening the stability of the First Edition doctrine.

---

## Vision

Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems (ETIS) is a repository-centered, evidence-driven engineering ecosystem for building, governing, operating, teaching, and stewarding trustworthy intelligent systems in the AI era.

ETIS now consists of:

- the ETIS Framework;
- the *Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems* Framework Reference Work;
- the ETIS Two-Volume Professional Edition;
- the ETIS Professional Computing Series;
- the ETIS Publications Program;
- eight professional appendices;
- the ETIS Educational Ecosystem;
- the ETIS Engineering Platform;
- two public repositories;
- downloadable books, educational products, and implementation resources;
- the Resource Center, ETIS Library, release history, and public roadmap.

ETIS is no longer only a publication.

It is a connected professional system for learning, applying, teaching, reviewing, governing, operating, and improving trustworthy intelligent systems.

---

## Foundational Principles

ETIS is built on durable engineering principles that should remain stable across future editions, publications, educational resources, and Platform capabilities.

- **AI proposes; engineers verify.**
- **Governance is architecture.**
- **Context is control.**
- **Everything important leaves evidence.**
- **The model is not the system.**
- **A demo is not operational proof.**
- **Humans cannot govern what they cannot understand.**
- **Trustworthiness is accumulated, not declared.**

These principles are constitutional anchors for the ecosystem.

Future work may extend ETIS, but it should not dilute these doctrines.

- **Publications Program** — focused, citable White Papers, Executive Briefs, and Education Papers.

A Professional Computing Series title does not revise the ETIS Framework Reference Work. Substantive doctrinal changes to the Framework belong in a future edition of the Framework Reference Work.

# Phase V — Future Editions and Long-Term Stewardship

**Status: Future**

Future editions should be driven by substantive doctrinal need rather than routine technology change.

## Potential Triggers

- material advances in agentic systems;
- durable changes in AI governance practice;
- regulatory developments with broad engineering consequences;
- accumulated educational and professional adoption evidence;
- mature operational lessons;
- significant improvements to the trustworthiness model;
- long-term stewardship experience.

## Future-Edition Principles

- preserve continuity with the First Edition;
- retain constitutional ETIS principles;
- distinguish corrections from doctrinal change;
- preserve publication and version history;
- avoid rewriting the framework for transient tools or vendors;
- require evidence before expanding doctrine.
