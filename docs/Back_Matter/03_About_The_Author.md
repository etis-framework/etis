# About the Author

## William T. O'Connell, Ph.D.

**IBM Distinguished Engineer · Former IBM Chief Technology Officer · University educator · Author and creator of ETIS**

William T. O'Connell, Ph.D., brings more than four decades of experience in software engineering, enterprise architecture, applied research, technical leadership, and engineering governance to **Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems (ETIS)**. His career includes Argonne National Laboratory, AT&T Bell Laboratories, more than 25 years at IBM, and more than three decades of university teaching.

He held **two IBM chief technology officer roles** and was recognized as an **IBM Distinguished Engineer**. His work has connected system architecture and technical strategy with the practical responsibilities of delivering, reviewing, operating, and governing complex software systems.

---

## Engineering and Technology Leadership

Dr. O'Connell began his career at Argonne National Laboratory and subsequently worked at AT&T Bell Laboratories on software and research in database systems, distributed computing, parallel processing, multimedia information systems, and large-scale architectures.

At IBM, which he joined in 1998, he worked across software, data management, analytics, enterprise architecture, and consulting. His leadership roles included chief technology responsibilities in IBM Software and IBM Consulting. In his final IBM position, he served as **Distinguished Engineer and Chief Technology Officer for Quality and Engineering Delivery Excellence** in IBM Consulting, helping assess engineering practices, architecture, delivery quality, governance, and operational risks across major client engagements in the Americas.

His experience includes architecture and engineering reviews, executive technology advice, quality and delivery assessments, and efforts to detect material technical risks before they become operational failures. The emphasis throughout has been on what engineers can demonstrate with evidence, not merely what a design or demonstration promises.

## Research, Innovation, and Education

Dr. O'Connell is an inventor or co-inventor on **11 United States patents** and has contributed technical publications, presentations, and conference and executive briefings on software engineering, data systems, enterprise architecture, and related technologies.

He holds a **Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from the Illinois Institute of Technology**, and a **B.S. in Computer Science, with a Mathematics minor, from North Central College**.

He has taught undergraduate and graduate computer science at institutions including Loyola University Chicago, Princeton University, the University of Toronto, Saint Francis University, Aurora University, and Joliet Junior College. At Loyola, his teaching includes software engineering and data structures and algorithms, with an emphasis on verification, engineering judgment, teamwork, responsible AI use, and repository-centered evidence.

## Books and ETIS Publications

Dr. O'Connell created ETIS to bring architecture, governance, operational trust, and evidence-based software engineering together as a coherent engineering discipline. Its central premise is that increasingly capable AI does not remove human engineering accountability: **AI proposes; engineers verify.**

His books include:

- **[Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems — Framework Reference Work](../Books/ETIS_Framework_Reference.md).** The integrated ETIS framework, available online and in a two-volume professional print edition, addresses the lifecycle from construction and release readiness through operations, governance, oversight, and stewardship.
- **[Engineering Agentic AI Systems](../Books/agentic-ai/index.md)** — *Build, Secure, Verify, and Operate Trustworthy AI Agents*. The featured ETIS Professional Computing Series title develops trusted execution boundaries, independently enforced authorization, recoverable agent workflows, security, verification, and six progressive Python reference builds.
- **[From Data Structures to Engineering Judgment](../Books/engineering-judgment/index.md)** — *Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems in the AI Era*. The inaugural Professional Computing Series title uses data structures and algorithms to develop enduring engineering judgment, evidence-based reasoning, and accountability.

The **Framework Reference Work** defines the broader discipline; the **Professional Computing Series** develops those engineering capabilities through focused domains. ETIS white papers, executive briefs, education papers, and companion resources provide additional entry points.

## Why ETIS Exists

Across research, enterprise delivery, governance reviews, and classrooms, Dr. O'Connell encountered a recurring challenge: capable technology does not by itself establish that a system is understandable, authorized, reliable, recoverable, or accountable.

ETIS responds by treating trustworthiness as an **engineered and verifiable system property**, not a claim about a model, vendor, or tool. It places architecture, context control, human responsibility, verification, operational evidence, and long-term stewardship at the center of software engineering in the AI era.

> In the AI era, engineering trust is the work.
