# Privacy

_Last updated: August 2026_

The ETIS website is intended to operate primarily as a static publication and resource site.

The site does not intentionally require reader accounts or collect personal information through comments, payment systems, or public user profiles.

---

## ETIS Privacy Philosophy

ETIS is built around engineering trust.

The website is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and tracking while providing access to the ETIS Framework, Framework Reference Work, ETIS Books, Publications Program, Educational Ecosystem, Engineering Platform, downloads, and repository resources.

Personal information should be collected only when there is a clear, legitimate purpose and an appropriate method for handling it.

---

## Hosting and Technical Logs

The site may be hosted through GitHub Pages or comparable static-site infrastructure.

Hosting, content-delivery, domain, or security providers may process standard technical information such as:

- IP address;
- browser and device information;
- requested pages;
- timestamps;
- referring pages;
- diagnostic, security, or access logs.

Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

---

## Analytics and Tracking

Current status: the ETIS publication site does not intentionally configure advertising trackers, cross-site behavioral tracking, or user-profiling tools.

If analytics or other measurement services are added, this page should be updated to identify the service, purpose, and data implications.

Browser, hosting, network, or external-service behavior may still create technical logs outside ETIS control.

---

## Email and Contact Information

When a reader contacts ETIS by email, the message may include personal information supplied by the sender, such as name, email address, institution, organization, and the contents of the inquiry.

That information may be used to:

- understand and respond to the inquiry;
- investigate a reported issue;
- evaluate a correction or erratum;
- address publication, educational, technical, or professional questions;
- preserve necessary correspondence records.

Do not send confidential, regulated, student-protected, client-sensitive, security-sensitive, export-controlled, or legally privileged information unless an appropriate communication channel has been established.

---

## Books, Downloads, and Publications

The ETIS website may provide access or links to book pages, PDFs, archives, repositories, and external publication or distribution platforms.

Accessing or downloading these resources may generate standard hosting or provider logs.

External platforms—including GitHub, Amazon, email providers, and other linked services—operate under their own privacy policies.

---

## External Links and Embedded Services

The site may link to external repositories, publications, standards, tools, organizations, or services.

ETIS does not control the privacy, security, accessibility, availability, or content practices of external sites.

Readers should review the policies of external services before providing personal information.

---

## Children and Student Information

The ETIS website is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Students and instructors should not publish protected student records, grades, personally identifiable educational records, confidential team information, or sensitive project data in public repositories or public ETIS communications.

Institutional users remain responsible for their own privacy, records, and educational-compliance obligations.

---

## Security

No website or email system can guarantee absolute security.

ETIS encourages responsible information sharing, minimal disclosure, and use of appropriate institutional or organizational channels for sensitive matters.

Security vulnerabilities affecting an external hosting or repository platform should also be reported through that platform's established process where appropriate.

---

## Changes to This Statement

This statement may be updated if the site adds analytics, forms, mailing lists, embedded services, new hosting arrangements, or other features that materially affect privacy.

The “Last updated” date should reflect substantive changes.
