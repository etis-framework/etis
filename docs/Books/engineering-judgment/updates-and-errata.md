## Stewardship Approach

Updates may include:

- factual corrections;
- wording clarifications;
- figure corrections;
- bibliographic or edition notes;
- and links to revised companion resources.

Substantive changes to the book's doctrine or architecture belong in a future edition rather than being silently rewritten through the website.
