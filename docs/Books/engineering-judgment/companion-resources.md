# Companion Resources

**For _From Data Structures to Engineering Judgment_**

The ETIS website is the authoritative home for the living resources associated with this book.

The book is intentionally finite. Companion resources can continue to evolve as the book is taught, applied, reviewed, and extended in professional practice.

---

## Resource Areas

Companion materials may include:

- chapter-aligned instructional material;
- exercises and case studies;
- repository starter kits;
- Engineering Note templates and examples;
- Model–Measure–Decide worksheets;
- Claim–Evidence–Boundary aids;
- AI-assisted engineering review checklists;
- educator resources;
- figure and presentation assets;
- updates and errata;
- and selected engineering tools.

---

## For Students and Independent Learners

Resources may support readers in turning chapter concepts into repeatable engineering practice through:

- quick-reference reasoning aids;
- Engineering Note examples;
- chapter-aligned exercises;
- decision and evidence worksheets;
- and selected implementation or case-study materials.

---

## For Educators

Educator resources may include:

- chapter-to-capability mappings;
- discussion and transfer questions;
- assignment and laboratory ideas;
- Engineering Note assessment guidance;
- classroom use of Claim–Evidence–Boundary and Model–Measure–Decide;
- and guidance for connecting foundational data structures and algorithms to software engineering practice.

---

## For Practicing Engineers and Engineering Teams

The same frameworks can be adapted as lightweight professional review disciplines for:

- design and architecture reviews;
- code and implementation review;
- performance and capacity investigations;
- benchmark review;
- production-readiness assessments;
- AI-assisted modification review;
- incident analysis;
- decision records;
- and technical mentoring.
