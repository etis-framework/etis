## Executive Perspective

AI changes the economics of producing software, not the responsibility for engineering dependable systems. Drawing on practitioner hiring and leadership experience, this paper explains five dimensions of professional potential—technical foundations, engineering judgment, learning agility, collaboration, and initiative supported by evidence—and how to build a living engineering portfolio that makes growth inspectable.

## What You Will Take Away

- Understand and demonstrate the five dimensions of professional potential.
- Develop one sustained, useful system through genuine releases and reviews.
- Use GitHub evidence—issues, pull requests, tests, decisions, and recovery—to show judgment.
- Prepare for deep technical interviews and evaluate mentoring opportunities.
- Treat professional development as a deliberate engineering program, beginning now.

## Who Should Read This Paper?

Second- through fourth-year students · Graduate students · Early-career engineers · Instructors · Mentors.
