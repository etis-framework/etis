# ETIS Publication Reading Paths

<p class="publication-lead">
The ETIS publication portfolio is designed as a connected body of work. These reading paths help readers enter the program through the sequence most relevant to their role, responsibilities, and goals.
</p>

## New to ETIS

**Recommended sequence:** WP-001 → WP-002 → WP-003 → WP-011 → WP-012

**Outcome:** Understand the industry shift, repository-centered engineering, engineering evidence, trust, and the core ETIS doctrine.

1. [WP-001 — Engineering Trustworthy Software in the AI Era](white-papers/wp-001.md)
2. [WP-002 — Repository-Centered Engineering](white-papers/wp-002.md)
3. [WP-003 — Engineering Evidence](white-papers/wp-003.md)
4. [WP-011 — Engineering Trust](white-papers/wp-011.md)
5. [WP-012 — The ETIS Manifesto](white-papers/wp-012.md)

## Technology Executive

**Recommended sequence:** EB-001 → EB-002 → EB-003 → EB-004 → EB-005

**Outcome:** Move from governance through organizational design, agentic readiness, platform strategy, and meaningful measurement.
