## Executive Perspective

Agentic AI creates extraordinary opportunity—and the possibility that a plausible model inference becomes a consequential business action at machine speed. This paper develops an architecture of governed autonomy: worker and supervisory agents operate within bounded authority; independent trusted services enforce policy and protect authoritative state; and evidence, containment, reconciliation, and recovery keep business outcomes accountable.

## What You Will Take Away

- Distinguish an agent assertion from an authorized, verified business outcome.
- Explain the two-gate execution boundary and why supervision is not security authority.
- Design authoritative state, evidence provenance, and bounded interventions.
- War-game excessive refunds, false completion, malicious telemetry, retries, and common-mode failures.
- Balance business value, control cost, assurance, and recoverability.
