# ETIS Engineering Platform

**Apply ETIS through staged, repository-centered engineering**

The ETIS Engineering Platform is the practical implementation environment for *Engineering Trustworthy Intelligent Systems*.

The ETIS Framework defines the discipline.  
The Two-Volume Edition provides its complete treatment.  
Publications provide focused guidance.  
The Engineering Platform turns ETIS into structured engineering work.

The Platform provides Engineering Stages ES-100 through ES-114, reusable templates, completed LMU/COICP reference examples, governance assets, and a Project Workspace for preserving project-specific engineering evidence.

## What the Engineering Platform Is

The Engineering Platform is the implementation companion to ETIS.

It is not a replacement for the Framework, the Two-Volume Edition, the Publications Program, or professional judgment.

It provides a structured environment in which teams can:

- move through the engineering lifecycle;
- understand what work should happen next;
- create evidence using reusable templates;
- study completed reference examples;
- make AI use visible and reviewable;
- prepare for reviews and readiness gates;
- preserve release and operational evidence;
- learn from incidents;
- steward systems after release.

The Platform is designed for real projects, course projects, professional pilots, institutional adoption, and reference implementations.

The Template Library provides reusable ETIS-aligned artifact structures for:

- review evidence;

### LMU / COICP Reference Examples

The Platform contains completed examples based on Lakeside Metropolitan University and the Campus Operations and Incident Coordination Platform.

These examples show what ETIS evidence can look like across a realistic lifecycle.

Use the examples to:

- understand artifact purpose;
- calibrate depth and quality;
- trace evidence across stages;
- study governance decisions;
- see how AI use is recorded and verified;
- connect construction evidence to release, operations, and stewardship.

The examples are reference models, not scripts to copy mechanically.

[Explore the LMU / COICP Examples ↗](https://platform.etisframework.org/examples/)

### Project Workspace

The Project Workspace gives teams a controlled place to create project-specific engineering evidence.

A typical workspace separates responsibilities:

```text
docs/      Think, decide, explain, and preserve evidence
src/       Build
tests/     Verify
data/      Support realistic use and evaluation
scripts/   Automate repeatable work
```

The workspace supports:

- student projects;
- professional teams;
- organizational pilots;
- internal reference implementations;
- engineering reviews;
- portfolio evidence.

The objective is not to fill folders.

The objective is to make important engineering work understandable, reviewable, governable, and durable.

[Explore the Project Workspace ↗](https://platform.etisframework.org/project-workspace/)

### Governance and Evidence Assets

The Platform makes governance operational through reusable assets for:

- AI-use disclosure;
- AI verification;
- authority and delegation boundaries;
- review-board evidence;
- risk and exception governance;
- release governance;
- operational readiness;
- repository stewardship;
- post-release learning.

Governance is not added after engineering work.

Within ETIS, governance is architecture.

## Who the Platform Serves

### Students

Use the Platform to practice professional engineering, produce reviewable evidence, use AI responsibly, defend decisions, and build credible portfolio material.

### Instructors

Use the Platform as a course project environment for staged assignments, phase gates, templates, reviews, release defenses, and professional formation.

### Engineering Teams

Use the Platform to establish a repeatable evidence architecture without inventing every lifecycle artifact from scratch.

### Architects and Technical Leads

Use the Platform to connect requirements, decisions, reviews, AI governance, release readiness, and operational evidence.

### Review Boards and Governance Leaders

Use the Platform to inspect claims, evidence, residual risk, ownership, authority, and readiness.

### Organizations

Use the Platform as a starting architecture for pilots, adoption programs, internal standards, engineering enablement, and trustworthy AI practices.

## Platform Boundary

The Engineering Platform is a reference implementation and adoption environment.

It does not:

- certify that a project is trustworthy;
- replace professional judgment;
- replace organizational governance;
- replace applicable law, regulation, policy, security review, or institutional requirements;
- guarantee that completing templates produces trustworthy outcomes.

Trustworthiness is earned through evidence, review, governance, operation, learning, and stewardship.
